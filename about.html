Hi, I'm Smita Pinjan

I am an experienced Business Intelligence/business analysis/data and AI Analyst specializing in transforming raw data into reliable, scalable, and trustworthy insights. My work sits at the intersection of analytics, data quality, data governance, and responsible AI — ensuring that dashboards, models, and metrics are accurate, explainable, and ready for confident decision making.
I bring a systems thinking mindset to every project. I diagnose inconsistencies, identify root causes, and ensure every insight is backed by clean, well-governed data. Whether supporting AI initiatives, enhancing BI reporting, or aligning analytics with compliance expectations, I focus on clarity, precision, and governance-aligned execution. My goal is to build data and AI solutions that leaders can trust, teams can scale, and organizations can rely on.
My experience spans the full analytics lifecycle: gathering requirements, data modelling, validating and automating data pipelines, building Power BI/Tableau dashboards, optimizing DAX models, engineering KPIs, and applying AI-assisted analysis for anomaly detection and decision support. I strengthen existing data systems by improving data quality, documentation, and lineage clarity.
I’m particularly drawn to environments where analytics and AI are evolving from traditional reporting into intelligent, proactive systems. I build solutions that don’t just visualize data — they detect patterns, surface risks, and enable smarter, faster decisions.
